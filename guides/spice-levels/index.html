LEVEL 3 · ON-PAGE SEX

Open Door

One or more sexual scenes appear on page. The activity is clear, but the scenes are usually limited enough that they do not dominate the book.

Expect a small number of complete scenes with recognizable physical detail.

WHAT IT DOESN’T TELL YOU

Open door describes visibility—not kink, darkness or emotional intensity.

QUICK ANSWERS

Spice rating FAQ

Six useful answers. No twelve-paragraph detour.

What is a spice rating in romance books? +

A spice rating estimates how explicitly sexual intimacy appears on the page, usually using a scale from low or closed-door romance to frequent, highly explicit scenes.

What do closed door and fade to black mean? +

Both keep explicit sexual activity off the page. A scene may be implied, begin and cut away, or resume after the intimacy has happened.

What does open door mean? +

Open door means sexual activity is shown on the page. The amount of detail and the number of scenes determine whether the book sits closer to level three, four or five.

Does a higher spice rating mean more romance? +

No. Spice measures visibility and explicitness, not emotional intensity, relationship quality, darkness, plot or how romantic the story feels.

Are spice ratings universal? +

No universal industry scale exists. smutHub uses this five-level framework consistently, but another reader, author or retailer may describe the same book differently.

Do spice ratings replace content warnings? +

No. Always check the individual book’s tags and content notes for specific acts, relationship dynamics, violence, darker themes or potential triggers.
