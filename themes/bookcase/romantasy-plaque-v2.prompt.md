# Romantasy plaque v2

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then converted from a flat
chroma-key background to transparent WebP for the bookshelf UI.

## Final prompt

Create a premium collectible hanging bookshelf plaque worthy of being sold as
a standalone digital cosmetic. Use a wide 3:1, angular carved-wood silhouette
with aged midnight-plum lacquer, worn antique-gold edging, visible painterly
texture, handmade irregularities, and asymmetrical thorned crimson and
dusty-rose flowers. Add a compact skull, sword, and dark ruby heart crest at
the upper center, contained entirely within the top 28 percent. Include a tiny
crescent moon, gemlike stars, curling black-green leaves, and delicate gold
filigree. Leave the broad center and lower-middle as an uninterrupted dark wood
field for live HTML personalization.

The medium should resemble richly detailed, hand-painted dark romantasy book
cover art with gouache and oil texture and worn gold-leaf accents—not clean
vector UI. Use blackened plum, oxblood, deep rose, bone ivory, antique gold,
midnight violet, and black-green. Show the complete object straight on with
generous padding. Include no text, letters, numbers, monograms, runes,
pseudo-writing, watermark, chains, or hanging hardware.

Generate on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` background with no shadow,
gradient, texture, reflection, glow, or floor plane so the object can be
extracted cleanly.
