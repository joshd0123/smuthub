# Library crest collection v1

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each crest was created on
a flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background, extracted locally, tightly cropped,
resized, and saved as a transparent lossless WebP. Names and mottos remain
live HTML so every crest can be personalized and stay accessible.

## Moonlit Cathedral

A wide blank personalized library plaque with a pointed Gothic-cathedral
silhouette, midnight-blue lacquered wood, antiqued silver tracery, moonflower
vines, sapphire stars, and one crescent jewel at the apex. The broad center is
kept dark and uninterrupted for live personalization. Cool moonlight, refined
3D heirloom materials, no text, hardware, skulls, weapons, or UI styling.

## Thornbound Reliquary

A wide faceted coffin-shield plaque for dark romance, murder, war, betrayal,
and dangerous fantasy. Blackened iron, charred ebony, oxblood enamel seams,
blade-scar gouges, thorn wire, raven-wing reliefs, and a compact broken-sword
and ruby emblem frame an almost-black empty center. Severe, adult, and refined;
no gore, text, skulls, or Halloween styling.

## Dragon's Oath

A wide tapered hexagonal plaque formed by two folded dragon wings. Charcoal
scales, burgundy velvet, champagne-gold ribs, ember gems, and a heart-shaped
negative space beneath a small crown surround a calm empty center. Premium 3D
collector-object treatment; no dragon character, text, fire, or game HUD.

## Founder's Key

A clean exclusive escutcheon for the Original 100. Black obsidian enamel,
precise stepped octagonal corners, a razor-thin antique-gold bezel, charcoal
leather, restrained key wards at the far ends, and one garnet beneath a minimal
crown. The design is rare through discipline rather than decoration. No text,
flowers, vines, skulls, weapons, wings, chains, or baroque clutter.

### Transparent frame v2

The Founder frame was refined as a precise object edit rather than regenerated:
the stepped obsidian-and-gold outer frame, crown, garnet, and key wards were
preserved while the entire leather center was removed to true transparency.
Tiny Roman `I` and `C` keeper marks and a restrained lower thorn engraving add
Original 100 provenance without turning the crest into costume jewelry. The
smoked crystal, depth, live name, reflection, and numbered key tag are rendered
as separate CSS planes so the reader's own library remains visible through it.

### Romantasy openwork v3

The final Founder direction replaces the severe architectural frame with an
openwork heirloom: blackened gold, hand-grown thorn vines, mature oxblood roses,
a crescent and pinprick stars, plus a garnet heart whose point becomes the key.
The entire center and every opening between the vines are true transparency.
Live personalized words sit over the ornament with only a translucent wine-dark
reading shadow behind them; no opaque plaque or simulated glass is baked in.
