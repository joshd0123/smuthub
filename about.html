The hub for romantasy readers.

smutHub exists to answer one question fast: is this book for me? Browse by trope, sort by heat, and see the content up front — so you know what you're getting into before page one.

Why we built it

Finding your next romantasy read usually means piecing together spoiler-filled reviews, cryptic BookTok acronyms, and vague heat descriptions. We wanted one place where every book is tagged the way readers actually think — the tropes, the spice level, the content warnings, the ending — and where the glossary decodes the genre's shorthand instead of assuming you already know it.

How we handle your data

We collect as little as possible. Browse freely and we store nothing about you; sign in (Google or a passwordless emailed link) and the only things we keep are your email and your bookshelf. No ads, no cross-site tracking, no selling your data. The details are in our Privacy Policy.
