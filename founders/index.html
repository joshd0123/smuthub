The honest part

Yes, AI helped build this. The goal is to undo that.

I'll say it plainly: AI helped me build smutHub, and it helped make the art you see. I'm not hiding that. But building it this way is how I finally got past the things I'd started and abandoned more times than I'd like to admit. It shipped. Now the job is to trade the AI work out for the real thing.

So here's the mechanism, and it's the reason anything on this site costs money at all:

The monthly art draw

Every month, one reader's artwork gets used to replace AI-generated images on the site — starting with book spines and covers. New winner, every month. Buy anything — the Founder's Key, a single item, the scan-to-add camera, even a coffee — and you're in that month's draw.

  • 1Any purchase enters you. One purchase in a month puts you in that month's draw.
  • 2$1 = 1 chance. Spend more and your odds go up — every dollar is another entry in the hat.
  • 3Winners get an Artist Alley. Your work goes on the site and we link straight out to your shop or store.
  • 4It's pointed away from AI. Every winner is one more piece of the site made by a person instead of a model.
⚿ Founders get lucky 13 — one guaranteed win + 13 free draws

Buy the Founder's Key and you're guaranteed a month with your art showcased when your number comes up, plus thirteen free draws on the house — a full year of monthly entries and a freebie on top. Spend beyond that and $1 = 1 more chance, same as everyone.

For the artists

The Artist Alley.

If you have a shop, a store, or a portfolio, a draw win gets you a spot in the Artist Alley — your name, your work on smutHub, and a link straight out to wherever you sell. It's a showcase, not a rights-grab: your art stays yours, and the Alley sends readers to you.

How it works: once payment is received for any product, I'll reach out to the creator directly to collect design specs and set up their Artist Alley page. No forms to chase — a real message from a real person.
